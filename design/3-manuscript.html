Site inspection — Bahnhofstrasse 14

10 AUGUST 2026 · F. BÖSIGER

The building was inspected on 10 August following the tenant's report of damp after heavy rain. Two defects were recorded, both on the exposed north and west elevations. Neither is structurally urgent, but both should be dealt with before the winter.

Findings

North wall

Hairline cracking runs approximately 30 cm below the second window. The crack is superficial, with no displacement along its length, which is consistent with settlement rather than ongoing movement.

  1. Monitor the crack over one further season.
  2. Fill and repaint once movement has been ruled out.

Roof, west gable

Two tiles above the west gable have slipped, leaving the underlay exposed. This is the most likely path for the water ingress the tenant described.

  1. Re-bed the two slipped tiles.
  2. Inspect the surrounding course for further movement.

Follow-up

  • Return visit before the first frost.
  • Confirm with the tenant that the damp has stopped.
